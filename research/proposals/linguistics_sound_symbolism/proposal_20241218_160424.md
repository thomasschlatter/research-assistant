"## Research Proposal: Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English\n\n### Abstract\nThis study investigates the relationship between sound features and word meanings in English, utilizing recent advancements in sound symbolism. By analyzing a large corpus of English words through phonetic, prosodic, and segmental feature extraction, we aim to develop machine learning models capable of predicting word meanings from their sounds.\n\n### Introduction\nSound symbolism, the association between specific sounds and meanings, remains a less understood phenomenon in linguistics. This study seeks to bridge the gap in understanding by examining the patterns between sound features and word meanings using a comprehensive corpus of English words.\n\n### Methodology\nWe will utilize the **WordNetLexicalRelations** dataset, known for its detailed lexical relationships among English words. Our analysis will involve:\n\n- **Feature Extraction**: Conducting phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis to identify sound features.\n- **Machine Learning Models**: Implementing neural networks and decision trees to predict word meanings based on these features.\n\n### Expected Outcomes\nThe research aims to pinpoint significant sound features influencing word meanings in English and to develop robust machine learning models for predicting word meanings from sounds. This will contribute to the ongoing discourse in linguistics regarding sound symbolism's impact on language structure and usage, offering valuable insights for natural language processing applications.\n\n### Significance\nBy integrating computational techniques with corpus analysis, this study provides a nuanced understanding of the intricate relationships between sound and meaning in English. It advances the field of sound symbolism in linguistics and opens new pathways for natural language processing innovations.\n\n### Data Source and Tools\n- **Data Source**: WordNetLexicalRelations dataset (No human subjects involved)\n- **Tools and Techniques**: Phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis; Neural networks; Decision trees\n"
