"## Research Proposal: Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English\n\n### Abstract\nThis study investigates the intricate relationship between sound features and word meanings in English, leveraging recent advancements in sound symbolism. Utilizing the WordNetLexicalRelations dataset, we aim to develop machine learning models that predict word meanings from their phonetic and prosodic features.\n\n### Introduction\nSound symbolism, the association between specific sounds and their meanings, remains an enigmatic aspect of linguistics. This study aims to elucidate this phenomenon by identifying patterns between sound features and word meanings within a comprehensive English word corpus.\n\n### Methodology\nWe will employ the **WordNetLexicalRelations** dataset for its extensive lexical relationships among English words. Our analysis will include:\n\n- **Feature Extraction**: Conducting detailed phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis to identify salient sound features.\n- **Machine Learning Models**: Developing and training neural networks and decision trees to predict word meanings based on extracted sound features.\n\n### Expected Outcomes\nThe research seeks to identify significant sound features that influence word meanings in English. We aim to develop robust machine learning models capable of predicting word meanings from sounds, contributing to the discourse on sound symbolism and its implications for language structure and processing.\n\n### Significance\nBy merging computational techniques with corpus analysis, this study offers a nuanced perspective on the relationship between sound and meaning in English. It advances sound symbolism research and paves new paths for innovations in natural language processing.\n\n### Data Source and Tools\n- **Data Source**: WordNetLexicalRelations dataset (No human subjects involved)\n- **Tools and Techniques**: Phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis; Neural networks; Decision trees"
