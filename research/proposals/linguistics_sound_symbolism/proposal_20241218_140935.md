"**Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English**\\n\\nAbstract:\\nThis study investigates the relationship between sound features and word meanings in English, building upon recent developments in sound symbolism. We analyze a large corpus of English words using feature extraction techniques such as phonetic and prosodic analysis, and develop machine learning models to predict word meanings from their sounds.\n\nIntroduction:\nSound symbolism is a linguistic phenomenon where certain sounds or sound combinations are associated with specific meanings. Despite its significance, the relationship between sound features and word meanings remains poorly understood in linguistics. This study aims to address this knowledge gap by investigating the patterns between sound features and word meanings using a large corpus of English words.\n\nMethodology:\nWe utilize the WordNet dataset, which provides lexical relationships between English words. We apply feature extraction techniques such as phonetic and prosodic analysis to analyze sound features. Machine learning models (e.g., neural networks, decision trees) are trained and evaluated on word meaning prediction tasks.\n\nExpected Outcomes:\nThis study aims to identify significant sound features influencing word meanings in English and develop accurate machine learning models for predicting word meanings from their sounds. This research addresses an ongoing debate in linguistics regarding the role of sound symbolism in language structure and usage, providing new insights for natural language processing applications.\n\nSignificance:\\nThis study combines computational methods with corpus analysis to shed light on complex relationships between sound and meaning in English. It contributes to a deeper understanding of sound symbolism in linguistics and provides new insights for natural language processing applications."
