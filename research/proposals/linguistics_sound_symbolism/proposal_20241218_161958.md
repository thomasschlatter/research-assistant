## Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English: A Computational Approach

### Abstract
This study investigates the intricate relationship between sound features and word meanings in English, leveraging recent advancements in sound symbolism. Utilizing the WordNetLexicalRelations dataset, we aim to develop machine learning models that predict word meanings from their phonetic and prosodic features.

### Introduction
Sound symbolism, the association between specific sounds and their meanings, remains an enigmatic aspect of linguistics. This study aims to elucidate this phenomenon by identifying patterns between sound features and word meanings within a comprehensive English word corpus.

### Literature Review
The field of sound symbolism explores how phonetic structures influence semantic interpretations. Previous studies have highlighted cross-linguistic prevalence of sound-meaning associations. However, robust computational analyses in English remain sparse, warranting further investigation.

### Methodology
We will employ the **WordNetLexicalRelations** dataset for its extensive lexical relationships among English words. Our analysis will include:

- **Feature Extraction**: Conducting detailed phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis using existing phonetic databases and software (e.g., Praat) to identify salient sound features.
- **Machine Learning Models**: Developing and training advanced neural networks (e.g., Convolutional Neural Networks for phonetic features) and decision tree ensembles (e.g., Random Forests) to predict word meanings based on extracted sound features. We will utilize Python libraries such as Scikit-learn and TensorFlow for model implementation.
- **Model Evaluation**: Evaluating model performance using standard metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, and comparing it against baseline models.

### Results
Expected results include identifying key sound features that influence word meanings and developing machine learning models with high predictive accuracy. Insights from these models will be statistically analyzed to ensure robustness and reliability.

### Discussion
The discussion will focus on the implications of identified sound features on linguistic theory and practical applications in natural language processing. We will explore how these findings can enhance text-to-speech systems and improve semantic search algorithms.

### Conclusion
This study advances the understanding of sound symbolism in English and provides insights for linguistic theory and NLP applications.
