"## Improved Proposal: Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English\n\n### Abstract\nThe objective of this study is to explore the intricate relationship between phonetic elements and the semantic properties of words in the English language, leveraging the latest advancements in the field of sound symbolism. By employing a comprehensive analysis of an extensive corpus of English lexicon through advanced feature extraction methodologies\u2014including phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analyses\u2014this research endeavors to construct predictive models using machine learning techniques to decipher word meanings based on their auditory characteristics.\n\n### Introduction\nSound symbolism presents a fascinating intersection of phonetics and semantics, where specific phonetic constructs are intrinsically linked to particular semantic interpretations. Despite its pervasive influence, the precise dynamics governing the interaction between sound features and word meanings remain elusive within linguistic scholarship. This research seeks to bridge this gap by systematically examining the associations between auditory features and semantic meanings, utilizing an expansive dataset of English vocabulary.\n\n### Methodology\nUtilizing the **WordNetLexicalRelations** dataset, noted for its comprehensive mapping of lexical interrelations among English vocabulary, this study applies sophisticated feature extraction techniques\u2014encompassing phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analyses\u2014to dissect sound features. Subsequently, predictive models such as neural networks and decision trees are developed and rigorously evaluated to forecast word meanings from their acoustic signatures.\n\n- **Data Source**: WordNetLexicalRelations dataset\n- **Feature Extraction**: Phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis\n- **Machine Learning Models**: Neural networks, decision trees\n\n### Expected Outcomes\nThrough this study, we anticipate elucidating key sound features that significantly influence word meanings in English, alongside the development of robust machine learning models capable of predicting semantic content from phonetic cues. This research contributes to the ongoing discourse in linguistics concerning the structural and functional significance of sound symbolism, offering novel insights for applications in natural language processing.\n\n### Significance\nBy integrating computational approaches with corpus-based linguistic analysis, this study aims to unravel the complex nexus between sound and meaning in English. It promises to advance the understanding of sound symbolism in linguistics while providing valuable contributions to the field of natural language processing."
