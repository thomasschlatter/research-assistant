"# Research Paper: Sound Symbolism and Word Meaning in English\n\n## Abstract\nThis study investigates the relationship between sound features and word meanings in English, utilizing recent advancements in sound symbolism. By analyzing a large corpus of English words through phonetic, prosodic, and segmental feature extraction, we aim to develop machine learning models capable of predicting word meanings from their sounds.\n\n## Introduction\nSound symbolism, the association between specific sounds and meanings, remains a less understood phenomenon in linguistics. This study seeks to bridge the gap in understanding by examining the patterns between sound features and word meanings using a comprehensive corpus of English words. By delving into sound symbolism, we aim to uncover the linguistic underpinnings that link phonetic expression with semantic interpretation.\n\n## Literature Review\nResearch on sound symbolism has shown that certain sounds can evoke specific meanings or emotional responses. Studies have explored the phonetic properties that contribute to this phenomenon, yet few have applied advanced machine learning techniques to predict word meanings. Our work builds on foundational studies to create a predictive model that furthers our understanding of this linguistic feature.\n\n## Methodology\nWe will utilize the **WordNetLexicalRelations** dataset, known for its detailed lexical relationships among English words. Our analysis will involve:\n\n- **Feature Extraction**: Conducting phonetic, prosodic, and segmental analysis to identify sound features that correlate with word meanings.\n- **Machine Learning Models**: Implementing neural networks and decision trees to predict word meanings based on extracted sound features.\n\n## Results\nWe anticipate that our models will identify key sound features that significantly influence word meanings, thereby validating our hypothesis regarding the predictive power of sound symbolism.\n\n## Discussion\nThe findings from this study have the potential to reshape our understanding of sound symbolism in linguistics. By demonstrating the ability to predict word meanings through sound features, we highlight the intricate relationship between phonetics and semantics, offering new insights into language processing and acquisition.\n\n## Conclusion\nThis research advances the field of sound symbolism by integrating computational methods with linguistic analysis, offering a new perspective on the influence of sound on meaning. The outcomes will contribute to natural language processing technologies and provide a deeper understanding of linguistic structures.\n\n## References\n- Hinton, L., Nichols, J., & Ohala, J. J.
