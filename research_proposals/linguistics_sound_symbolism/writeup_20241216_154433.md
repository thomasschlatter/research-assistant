**Introduction**

Linguistic isolation, resulting from geographical or cultural factors, can lead to distinct sound symbolic patterns within endangered languages. Despite significant efforts to document and analyze these languages, a comprehensive understanding of their phonological properties and sound symbolic tendencies remains elusive. This study aims to investigate the phonological consequences of linguistic isolation on sound symbolism in endangered languages using large-scale datasets.

**Objectives**

1. To identify sound symbolic patterns in endangered languages using GLD (Global Language Database) and ISO639-3 (Language Codes Standard) datasets.
2. To analyze the phonological consequences of linguistic isolation on these sound symbolic patterns.
3. To compare our findings with those from well-documented languages.
4. To investigate the relationship between language contact, linguistic change, and sound symbolism.

**Methodology**

This study will employ a quantitative analysis using machine learning techniques (e.g., Random Forests) to identify patterns in the datasets, utilizing phonological features as predictors for abstract concepts like time, space, and causality. Statistical analysis (ANOVA) will be used to compare our findings across different language families and levels of linguistic isolation.

**Expected Outcomes**

This study is expected to contribute significantly to the field of sound symbolism by providing a comprehensive understanding of how linguistic isolation influences phonological representation across endangered languages. Our research will shed light on the relationship between language evolution, cultural context, and cognitive processes.

**Potential Impact**

The findings of this study have the potential to impact various fields, including:

1. **Linguistics**: This study will contribute to a better understanding of sound symbolism in endangered languages, shedding light on the phonological consequences of linguistic isolation.
2. **Cognitive Science**: The research will explore the relationship between language evolution, cultural context, and cognitive processes, providing insights into how humans perceive and represent abstract concepts.
3. **Education**: The study's findings can inform education policies and practices related to endangered languages, promoting their preservation and revitalization.

**Conclusion**

This study has the potential to make a significant contribution to the field of sound symbolism by providing a comprehensive understanding of how linguistic isolation influences phonological representation across endangered languages. Our research will shed light on the relationship between language evolution, cultural context, and cognitive processes, with potential implications for linguistics, cognitive science, and education.
