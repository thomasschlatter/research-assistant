**Title:** Exploring Cross-linguistic Sound Symbolism Patterns Through Computational Analysis of Established Databases

**Introduction:**
Sound symbolism refers to the inherent relationship between the sound of a word and its meaning, which is often independent of linguistic convention. While the concept of sound symbolism has intrigued linguists for decades, there is still a need for comprehensive cross-linguistic studies to uncover universal patterns and language-specific variations. Recent advancements in computational linguistics provide an opportunity to analyze large-scale datasets and explore sound symbolism associations across languages.

**Objectives:**
1. Identify universal sound symbolism patterns across languages.
2. Investigate language-specific variations in sound symbolism associations.
3. Explore the relationship between phonetic features and semantic domains in sound symbolism.
4. Validate the findings against existing linguistic theories on sound symbolism.

**Methodology:**
1. **Corpus Selection:** Utilize the World Atlas of Language Structures (WALS) database for phonetic and phonological information.
2. **Data Processing:** Extract sound symbolism-related words from the ASJP Database and the World Loanword Database.
3. **Feature Extraction:** Use phonetic and phonological features to quantify sound symbolism associations.
4. **Computational Analysis:** Apply machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and correlations in sound symbolism data.
5. **Statistical Validation:** Conduct statistical tests to validate the significance of the identified patterns.

**Expected Outcomes:**
1. Comprehensive analysis of sound symbolism patterns across languages.
2. Identification of universal and language-specific trends in sound symbolism.
3. Insights into the relationship between phonetic features and semantic domains in sound symbolism.
4. Validation of computational methods for studying sound symbolism on a large scale.

**Potential Impact:**
This research aims to bridge the gap in cross-linguistic sound symbolism studies by leveraging computational analysis on established linguistic datasets. By uncovering universal and language-specific patterns, this study has the potential to significantly advance our understanding of sound symbolism across languages. The findings may contribute to linguistic theories on sound symbolism and have implications for fields such as cognitive science and natural language processing. 

In conclusion, this research holds promise for shedding light on the intricate relationship between sound and meaning in language, paving the way for future studies in this fascinating area of linguistics.
