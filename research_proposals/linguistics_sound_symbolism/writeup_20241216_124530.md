**Introduction**

Sound symbolism, the phenomenon where sounds in language are non-arbitrarily linked to meanings, has fascinated linguists and cognitive scientists for decades. While individual studies have delved into sound symbolism in specific languages, a comprehensive cross-linguistic analysis is lacking. This research proposal aims to bridge this gap by employing a computational approach to uncover universal patterns in sound symbolism across multiple languages.

**Objectives**

1. **Identification of Sound Symbolic Words**: The primary goal is to identify sound symbolic words in various languages using established linguistic databases.
   
2. **Phonetic Analysis**: Analyzing the phonetic features of sound symbolic words to identify common patterns that transcend linguistic boundaries.
   
3. **Semantic Exploration**: Investigating the semantic domains where sound symbolism is prevalent and examining cultural influences on sound symbolic associations.
   
4. **Model Development**: Developing computational models to predict sound symbolism in new words based on linguistic features extracted from existing datasets.

**Methodology**

1. **Data Collection**: Publicly available linguistic databases like the World Atlas of Language Structures (WALS) and the Phonetics Information Base and Lexicon (PHOIBLE) will be utilized to compile a diverse set of sound symbolic words from various languages.
   
2. **Phonetic Analysis**: Extraction of phonetic features from sound symbolic words, including consonant-vowel patterns, phoneme frequencies, and acoustic properties.
   
3. **Semantic Analysis**: Categorization of sound symbolic words based on their semantic domains and comparison across different languages to identify commonalities and differences.
   
4. **Machine Learning Modeling**: Training machine learning algorithms on linguistic features to predict sound symbolism in new words, thereby automating the identification process.

**Expected Outcomes**

1. **Comprehensive Analysis**: The research aims to provide a comprehensive analysis of sound symbolism, elucidating both universal and language-specific patterns.
   
2. **Insights into Cognitive Mechanisms**: By exploring sound symbolism, the study seeks to offer insights into the cognitive mechanisms that underlie this linguistic phenomenon and its implications for language evolution.
   
3. **Computational Tools**: The development of computational tools for predicting sound symbolism in diverse linguistic contexts will have implications for fields like natural language processing and cognitive science.

**Potential Impact**

By leveraging publicly available datasets, this research ensures compliance with ethical guidelines and negates the need for human subject research. The findings of this study are expected to advance our understanding of sound symbolism, contributing to the broader discourse on language and cognition. The developed computational models hold promise for practical applications in various domains, including machine translation, language learning tools, and cognitive science research.
