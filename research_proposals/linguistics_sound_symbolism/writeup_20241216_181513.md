**Unpacking the Phonosemantics of Affective Sounds in World Languages: An Exploratory Analysis of Sound Symbolism using the Open Multilingual Lexicon (OML)**

**Introduction**

While significant progress has been made in sound symbolism research, there remains a gap in understanding the phonosemantic relationships between affective sounds and their corresponding emotions across languages. This study aims to address this knowledge gap by conducting an exploratory analysis of sound symbolism using the Open Multilingual Lexicon (OML) dataset.

**Objectives**

This research seeks to achieve three primary objectives:

1. **Investigate the distribution and frequencies of affective sounds**: Using the OML dataset, we will examine how often and in which languages affective sounds (e.g., "ah," "oh," "um") occur.
2. **Identify phonosemantic patterns between affective sounds and emotions**: Focusing on consonant-vowel combinations, diphthongs, and syllable structures, we will explore the relationships between affective sounds and emotions across languages.
3. **Examine the relationship between sound symbolism and linguistic typology**: By investigating how different language families and grammatical structures influence the perception of affective sounds, we aim to shed light on the cognitive basis of sound symbolism.

**Methodology**

This study will employ a mixed-methods approach, combining quantitative and qualitative methods to achieve its objectives. The methodology includes:

1. **Dataset selection**: The OML dataset will be used exclusively for this research.
2. **Data preparation**: Affective sounds will be extracted from the OML dataset using Python scripts and libraries (e.g., NLTK, spaCy).
3. **Phonosemantic pattern identification**: Multivariate statistical methods (e.g., PCA, cluster analysis) and machine learning algorithms (e.g., random forests, neural networks) will be employed to identify patterns in affective sounds across languages.
4. **Cognitive modeling**: A cognitive model of sound symbolism will be developed using the results from the phonosemantic pattern identification step.

**Expected Outcomes**

This research is expected to contribute significantly to our understanding of sound symbolism by:

1. **Providing a comprehensive analysis of affective sounds**: The study will shed light on the distribution and frequencies of affective sounds across languages.
2. **Identifying phonosemantic patterns between affective sounds and emotions**: By exploring the relationships between affective sounds and emotions, we aim to provide insights into the cognitive basis of sound symbolism.
3. **Shedding light on the relationship between sound symbolism and linguistic typology**: This research will examine how different language families and grammatical structures influence the perception of affective sounds.

**Potential Impact**

This study has the potential to impact various fields, including:

1. **Linguistics**: By providing a comprehensive analysis of affective sounds across languages, this research will contribute to our understanding of sound symbolism.
2. **Cognitive Science**: The study's findings on phonosemantic patterns and cognitive modeling will shed light on the cognitive basis of sound symbolism.
