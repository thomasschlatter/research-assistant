**Introduction:**

Sound Symbolism, an area within Linguistics, examines how sound patterns influence word meanings. Recent studies have shown that certain sound patterns can convey semantic nuances, such as animacy or affective value. However, the application of Sound Symbolism to lexical acquisition remains a significant research gap. This study aims to address this issue by investigating whether sound symbolism can predict the emergence of novel words in languages.

**Objectives:**

This study has three primary objectives:

1. **To develop a novel predictive model**: Develop a predictive model that exploits sound symbolism in lexical acquisition.
2. **To investigate the predictive power of sound symbolism**: Investigate the extent to which sound symbolism can predict the emergence of novel words in languages.
3. **To contribute to ongoing research on Sound Symbolism**: Contribute to the ongoing research on Sound Symbolism by providing insights into its application in lexical acquisition.

This study will employ a large-scale analysis using the Open Multilingual Wordnet (OMW) dataset, which provides extensive lexical resources for numerous languages. The methodology involves four stages:

1. **Data preparation**: Utilize the OMW dataset and ensure that only publicly available data is used.
2. **Feature extraction**: Extract sound symbolic features from the OMW data using a combination of manual and automated methods.
3. **Model development**: Develop a predictive model to forecast new word formations based on sound symbolic patterns.
4. **Evaluation**: Evaluate the performance of the predictive model using metrics such as precision, recall, and F1 score.
