**Title: "Exploring the Role of Sound Symbolism in Cross-Linguistic Word Perception: A Computational Analysis Using Existing Datasets"**

**Introduction:**
Sound symbolism is a linguistic phenomenon where sounds are associated with specific meanings, playing a crucial role in word perception. This research proposal aims to delve into the intricate relationship between sound symbolism and word perception across diverse languages. Despite advancements in this field, there is still a significant gap in understanding how sound symbolic patterns influence word perception across different linguistic backgrounds.

**Objectives:**
1. Analyze sound symbolic patterns in various languages using existing sound symbolism databases.
2. Develop computational models to predict sound symbolism in words across languages.
3. Investigate the impact of sound symbolism on word perception in multilingual contexts.
4. Determine the consistency or variation of sound symbolism across languages and language families.

**Methodology:**
1. **Data Collection:** Utilize sound symbolism databases like the "Sound Symbolism Databank" and "Phoible Database" to collect phonetic and semantic information from multiple languages.
2. **Feature Extraction:** Extract acoustic and semantic features from the collected data.
3. **Computational Modeling:** Develop machine learning models to predict sound symbolism based on acoustic and semantic features.
4. **Cross-Linguistic Analysis:** Apply models to analyze sound symbolism across different languages and explore the consistency of sound-meaning associations.
5. **Evaluation:** Assess model performance through simulations and statistical analyses to understand sound symbolism's impact on word perception.

**Expected Outcomes:**
1. Insights into sound symbolism mechanisms in word perception across languages.
2. Development of computational models for cross-linguistic sound symbolism analysis.
3. Implications for psycholinguistics, cognitive science, and computational linguistics by revealing sound symbolic patterns' universality or variability.

**Potential Impact:**
This research will advance the understanding of sound symbolism in linguistics, contributing to the broader discourse on the interplay between sound and meaning in language. The computational approach adopted in this study can pave the way for innovative research in cross-linguistic sound symbolism studies. Moreover, the findings could have practical applications in fields related to language processing and cognition.
