**Title:** Investigating the Role of Prosody in Sound Symbolism: A Quantitative Analysis of Rhythmic Patterns across Languages using the Open Multilingual WordNet (OMW) Database

**Introduction**

Sound Symbolism, a phenomenon where sound patterns are associated with meaning, has garnered significant attention in Linguistics research. However, previous studies have primarily focused on phonological and lexical aspects, leaving the role of prosody largely unexplored. Recent advances in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and multimodal analysis have made it possible to investigate the rhythmic properties of languages. This study aims to bridge the gap in understanding how prosodic features contribute to symbolic associations across languages.

**Objectives**

1. To quantify the prosodic features of words containing sound symbolic associations.
2. To investigate whether specific prosodic patterns are linked to particular sound symbolic meanings across languages.
3. To compare the relative importance of phonological and prosodic factors in shaping sound symbolism.

**Methodology**

This study will utilize the Open Multilingual WordNet (OMW) database, a publicly available online repository containing lexical data for over 150 languages. A subset of OMW entries with sound symbolic associations will be extracted and analyzed for prosodic features using automated tools. A mixed-effects model will be employed to examine the relationship between prosodic characteristics, sound symbolism, and language-specific patterns. Interactive visualizations will facilitate exploration and interpretation of results.

**Expected Outcomes**

This study expects to find that specific prosodic characteristics (e.g., stress, syllable structure) are systematically associated with certain sounds or sound combinations, potentially influencing sound symbolism. The findings are expected to contribute to a deeper understanding of Sound Symbolism by incorporating prosody as a critical aspect of linguistic analysis.

**Potential Impact**

The findings of this study have potential implications for:

1. **Cross-linguistic comparison:** Understanding how prosody influences sound symbolism across languages can inform the development of more nuanced cross-linguistic theories.
2. **Sound symbolism in machine learning:** This research can provide insights into how to incorporate prosodic features in NLP models, potentially improving language processing and generation capabilities.

**Conclusion**

This study aims to bridge the gap in understanding how prosodic features contribute to symbolic associations across languages. By providing insights into the rhythmic properties associated with sound symbolic meanings, this research aims to inform theoretical models of sound symbolism and its role in language acquisition and processing. The findings are expected to have significant implications for cross-linguistic comparison and sound symbolism in machine learning.
