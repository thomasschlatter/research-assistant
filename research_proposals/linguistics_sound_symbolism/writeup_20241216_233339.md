**Investigating the Effects of Phonetic Markedness on Sound Symbolic Associations in Languages with Complex Consonantal Systems: A Corpus-Based Study using the 2023 Kaggle "Large-scale Phoneme Dataset"**

**Introduction:**
Sound symbolism, a concept in which sounds evoke certain meanings or associations, has garnered significant attention in recent years. However, despite considerable advances in this area, there remains a lack of understanding about how phonetic markedness (the presence or absence of distinctive features) influences sound symbolic associations, particularly in languages with complex consonantal systems. This research aims to fill this knowledge gap by investigating the relationship between phonetic markedness and sound symbolic associations in such languages.

**Objectives:**

1. To investigate the relationship between phonetic markedness and sound symbolic associations in languages with complex consonantal systems.
2. To identify specific sound-symbolic patterns that are sensitive to markedness and explore their linguistic and cognitive implications.

**Methodology:**
This study will employ a corpus-based approach using the 2023 Kaggle "Large-scale Phoneme Dataset", which provides comprehensive phonetic transcriptions for over 1,000 languages. The dataset's large size and diverse language coverage make it an ideal choice for investigating sound symbolic patterns across languages with varying levels of markedness.

**Data Preparation:**

* Preprocessing the Kaggle dataset to create a phonetically consistent representation.
* Identifying languages with complex consonantal systems (e.g., those with 50+ distinct consonant phonemes).
* Extracting relevant sound-symbolic patterns from the dataset using a combination of statistical and machine learning techniques.

**Expected Outcomes:**
This research is expected to provide novel insights into the relationship between phonetic markedness and sound symbolic associations in languages with complex consonantal systems. The study will explore specific sound-symbolic patterns that are sensitive to markedness, shedding light on their linguistic and cognitive implications.

**Potential Impact:**

* This study has the potential to contribute significantly to our understanding of the intricate connections between phonology, semantics, and cognition.
* By exploring these relationships in a systematic and corpus-based manner, this research can inform theories of sound symbolism and language processing.
* The findings from this study may also have implications for fields beyond linguistics, such as musicology, anthropology, and cognitive science.

This research aims to fill the knowledge gap regarding the relationship between phonetic markedness and sound symbolic associations in languages with complex consonantal systems.
