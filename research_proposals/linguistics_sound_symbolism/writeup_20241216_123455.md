**Title: "Exploring Cross-Linguistic Patterns of Sound Symbolism in Animal Words: A Computational Analysis Using Publicly Available Datasets"**

**Introduction:**
Sound Symbolism, the non-arbitrary association of sounds with meanings, is a captivating aspect of linguistic research. While significant progress has been made in understanding sound symbolism in human-made objects and actions, its presence in animal words across different languages remains relatively unexplored. This research aims to delve into the cross-linguistic consistency and variability of sound symbolism in animal vocabulary.

**Objectives:**
1. Identify sound symbolic patterns in animal words across diverse languages.
2. Investigate the relationship between phonetic features and semantic associations in animal terms.
3. Determine the extent of cross-linguistic consistency in sound symbolism related to animals.
4. Explore potential evolutionary and cognitive factors influencing sound symbolism in animal vocabulary.

**Methodology:**
- **Data Collection:** Utilize publicly available lexical databases to gather animal words in multiple languages.
- **Phonetic Analysis:** Extract phonetic features and categorize them based on sound symbolism principles.
- **Semantic Analysis:** Identify semantic features associated with each animal word and analyze cross-modal associations between sounds and meanings.
- **Statistical Modeling:** Apply computational models like clustering algorithms and regression analysis to identify sound symbolism patterns.
- **Cross-Linguistic Comparison:** Compare sound symbolic patterns across languages and animal categories to assess cross-linguistic consistency.

**Expected Outcomes:**
1. Provide a comprehensive analysis of sound symbolism in animal vocabulary across diverse languages.
2. Offer insights into the mechanisms of sound symbolism concerning animal characteristics.
3. Enhance understanding of cross-linguistic variations and universality of sound symbolism in animal terms.
4. Contribute to theoretical frameworks of sound symbolism and its evolutionary significance in language evolution.

**Potential Impact:**
By shedding light on the intricate relationship between sound symbolism and animal vocabulary, this research will advance the field of Linguistics. It will offer new perspectives on the universality of linguistic phenomena across cultures and contribute to a deeper understanding of how sound symbolism operates in the domain of animal words.

This study, conducted ethically using publicly available datasets, aims to fill a gap in existing research and pave the way for further exploration into the fascinating world of sound symbolism in language.
