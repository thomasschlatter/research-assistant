Here is a structured write-up of the research proposal:

**Introduction**

Sound symbolism is a fundamental aspect of linguistic expression that refers to the use of sound patterns to convey meaning and emotions. Despite significant progress in studying this phenomenon, there remains a pressing need to understand how phonetic features (e.g., voicing, nasality) contribute to sound symbolism across languages. This research aims to investigate the role of phonetic features in shaping cross-linguistic sound symbolism patterns.

**Objectives**

This study seeks to achieve three primary objectives:

1. **Develop a novel computational framework**: Utilize techniques from machine learning (ML) and natural language processing (NLP) to analyze phonetic feature co-occurrences across languages.
2. **Investigate phonetic feature associations with symbolic meaning**: Examine how specific phonetic features are linked to sound symbolism patterns in various linguistic contexts, including semantic fields (e.g., emotions, actions).
3. **Provide insights into the universality of sound symbolism**: Investigate whether phonetic feature-driven sound symbolism patterns exhibit cross-linguistic similarities or differences.

**Methodology**

This study will employ a mixed-methods approach to achieve its objectives:

1. **Dataset selection**: Utilize the OpenSpeech database (2023-2024), a comprehensive online repository containing speech sounds from over 100 languages.
2. **Feature extraction**: Apply phonetic feature analysis techniques to the selected dataset, focusing on voicing, nasality, and other relevant features.
3. **Machine learning modeling**: Employ ML algorithms to identify patterns in phonetic feature co-occurrences and their relationships with symbolic meaning across languages.
4. **Validation**: Perform thorough validation using cross-validation methods and statistical tests.

**Expected Outcomes**

This research aims to:

1. **Contribute to a deeper understanding of sound symbolism**: Provide novel insights into the role of phonetic features in shaping cross-linguistic sound symbolism patterns.
2. **Advance computational methodologies**: Develop and apply innovative techniques for analyzing phonetic feature associations with symbolic meaning.

**Potential Impact**

The findings of this research have the potential to:

1. **Inform language teaching and learning**: Provide insights into how phonetic features can be used to convey meaning in language instruction, promoting more effective language acquisition.
2. **Influence linguistic theory and practice**: Contribute to a deeper understanding of sound symbolism and its role in shaping linguistic expression, with implications for theoretical frameworks and practical applications.

**Human Subjects Research Exemption**

This research proposal involves no human subject research, relying exclusively on publicly available datasets from the OpenSpeech database (2023-2024).
